How to enter numbers
  • Feet mode (the default): type the feet, then one key for inches (0–11), then one key for sixteenths (0–15). 1 0 0 0 → 10′-0 0/16. 2 4 5 10 → 24′-5 10/16, which becomes 24′-5 5/8″ when you press =.
  • After a length, × and ÷ take a plain number: 1 0 0 0 ÷ 3 → 3′-4″.
  • FT/DEC switches to plain decimal numbers. In either mode you can still use unit keys: 12 Feet, 3 ⁄ 8 Inch, 2 m.
  • 12 Feet Feet → 12 sq ft (press 3× for cu ft)
  • Press a unit key on a result to convert it. Press Feet or Inch again for decimals.
  • Enter any two of Rise Run Slope Pitch, then press another one to solve it. 1000 Rise 5000 Run Slope → 50′-11 7/8″.
  • Enter total floor-to-floor rise and press Stair repeatedly. Enter a length and press Circ for circumference and area.
  • Keyboard: digits, + − * /, ' feet, " inch, \ fraction, d FT/DEC, Enter, Esc, Backspace. Keys 10–15 need the on-screen buttons in Feet mode.
