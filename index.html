How to enter numbers
  • 5 Feet 6 Inch 3 ⁄ 8 → 5′ 6-3/8″
  • 12 Feet Feet → 12 sq ft (press 3× for cu ft)
  • Press a unit key on a result to convert it. Press Feet or Inch again for decimals.
  • Enter any two of Rise Run Diag Pitch, then press another one to solve it.
  • Enter total floor-to-floor rise and press Stair repeatedly. Enter a length and press Circ for circumference and area.
  • Keyboard: digits, + − * /, ' feet, " inch, \ fraction, Enter, Esc, Backspace.
